# Mapping ArcKit to ITIL Service Management Processes

The integration of ArcKit with ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) Service Management Processes represents a strategic convergence of architecture governance and IT service management best practices. As organizations adopt AI-driven development at scale, aligning ArcKit's architecture-as-code philosophy with ITIL's structured service management framework enables enterprises to achieve both technical consistency and operational excellence. In 2026, with the emergence of ITIL 5 and the growing emphasis on AI governance, this integration has become essential for organizations seeking to maintain control over their multi-platform LLM deployments while adhering to established service management standards.

#### The Convergence of Architecture Governance and IT Service Management

The relationship between ArcKit and ITIL represents a natural evolution in enterprise governance. Where ITIL traditionally focused on service delivery and support processes, ArcKit extends governance into the realm of architecture decisions and AI-assisted development. Together, they provide a comprehensive framework that addresses both the "what" (architectural standards and patterns) and the "how" (service management processes and procedures) of enterprise AI adoption.

**Synergies Between ArcKit and ITIL:**
- **Governance Alignment**: Both frameworks emphasize the importance of governance, controls, and standardized processes
- **Documentation Standards**: ArcKit's architecture-as-code approach complements ITIL's documentation requirements
- **Process Integration**: ArcKit's workflows can be integrated with ITIL processes for seamless end-to-end management
- **Continuous Improvement**: Both frameworks promote iterative improvement and learning from experience
- **Risk Management**: Shared focus on identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks across the enterprise

**Evolution to ITIL 5:**
ITIL 5, expected to roll out updates in the second half of 2026, represents a significant evolution in service management thinking. The new version aligns terminology and incorporates established AI use cases, including specific guidance on AI governance. This reflects the growing recognition that AI systems, including LLM platforms, require specialized governance approaches that extend beyond traditional IT service management.

> The most successful organizations are those that recognize that AI adoption is not just a technical challenge but a governance transformation. ArcKit provides the architectural governance layer, while ITIL provides the service management framework. Together, they create a comprehensive governance ecosystem that ensures AI capabilities are delivered as reliable, well-managed services.

#### ITIL Service Management Processes Overview

To effectively map ArcKit to ITIL, it's essential to understand the core ITIL service management processes and how they relate to architecture governance. ITIL 4 (and emerging ITIL 5) organizes service management around the Service Value System and Service Value Chain.

**ITIL Service Value System Components:**
- **Guiding Principles**: Recommendations for decision-making and action
- **Governance**: Ensure policies, controls, and strategies are properly defined and implemented
- **Service Value Chain**: Operational model for creating value through service management
- **Practices**: Sets of organizational resources designed for performing work or accomplishing objectives
- **Continuous Improvement**: Recurring organizational activity to enhance performance at all levels

**ITIL Service Value Chain Activities:**
1. **Plan**: Ensure a shared understanding of the vision, current status, and improvement direction
2. **Improve**: Ensure continual improvement of products, services, and practices
3. **Engage**: Ensure good relationships with all stakeholders
4. **Design & Transition**: Ensure products and services continually meet stakeholder expectations
5. **Obtain/Build**: Ensure service components are available when and where needed
6. **Deliver & Support**: Ensure services are delivered and supported according to agreed specifications

**Key ITIL Practices Relevant to ArcKit:**
- **Service Level Management**: Defining, negotiating, and managing service levels for AI platforms
- **Change Control**: Managing changes to architecture standards and AI configurations
- **Incident Management**: Handling architecture-related incidents and issues
- **Problem Management**: Identifying and resolving root causes of architectural problems
- **Configuration Management**: Managing architecture configurations and dependencies
- **Knowledge Management**: Capturing and sharing architectural knowledge and best practices
- **Monitoring & Event Management**: Monitoring architecture compliance and platform performance

#### Detailed Process Integration: Change Management

**Service Transition: Change Management for Architecture Governance**
One of the most critical integrations is between ArcKit's architecture governance and ITIL's Change Management practice, ensuring that changes to architectural standards and AI configurations are properly controlled and coordinated.

**Change Management Process Flow:**
1. **Change Identification**: ArcKit identifies needed changes to architecture standards or platform configurations
2. **Change Request**: Formal change request created in ITIL system with ArcKit context
3. **Impact Assessment**: ArcKit provides impact analysis of proposed changes on existing systems
4. **Change Evaluation**: ITIL Change Evaluation process considers ArcKit's architectural impact assessment
5. **Change Authorization**: ITIL Change Advisory Board (CAB) approves changes with ArcKit input
6. **Change Implementation**: ArcKit implements changes with ITIL coordination and oversight
7. **Change Review**: Post-implementation review ensures changes achieved desired architectural outcomes

**Change Types and ArcKit Integration:**
- **Standard Changes**: Pre-approved ArcKit changes (e.g., template updates, minor pattern refinements)
- **Normal Changes**: ArcKit changes requiring assessment and approval (e.g., new architecture standards)
- **Emergency Changes**: Urgent ArcKit changes with expedited review (e.g., security patches, critical compliance updates)

#### Service Level Management for AI Platforms

**Service Level Management: Defining and Managing AI Service Levels**
ITIL's Service Level Management practice provides the framework for defining, negotiating, and managing the service levels of AI platforms integrated with ArcKit governance.

**Service Level Definitions:**
- **Availability**: Percentage of time AI platforms are available for architecture governance activities
- **Performance**: Response times, throughput, and reliability of AI platform operations
- **Capacity**: Ability of AI platforms to handle architecture validation and governance workloads
- **Continuity**: Ability to maintain architecture governance during disruptions
- **Security**: Compliance with security requirements and data protection standards

**Service Level Agreement (SLA) Components:**
