# Specialized Platforms (Healthcare, Finance, Government)

Industry-specific LLM platforms have emerged as critical solutions for sectors with stringent regulatory requirements, unique operational constraints, and specialized domain knowledge. Healthcare, finance, and government organizations face distinctive challenges that generic LLM platforms often cannot adequately address. Specialized platforms tailored for these sectors provide the necessary compliance frameworks, security controls, and domain-specific capabilities that enable safe and effective AI adoption within heavily regulated environments.

#### The Regulatory Landscape Driving Specialization

The regulatory environment in 2026 has become increasingly complex, with sector-specific requirements that demand specialized LLM solutions. Understanding this landscape is essential for selecting and deploying appropriate platforms.

**Healthcare Regulatory Framework:**
- **HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)**: US healthcare privacy and security regulations requiring protected health information (PHI) safeguards
- **HITRUST**: Healthcare-specific security framework with comprehensive compliance requirements
- **GDPR**: European data protection regulations with additional healthcare-specific provisions
- **MHRA (UK)**: Medical device regulations including AI as a Medical Device (AIaMD) framework
- **CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services)**: US healthcare program compliance requirements
- **State-Level Regulations**: Additional requirements in states like California (CCPA), New York, and others

**Finance Regulatory Framework:**
- **GLBA (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act)**: US financial privacy requirements
- **SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act)**: Financial reporting and audit requirements
- **Dodd-Frank**: Financial reform regulations with data governance implications
- **PCI DSS**: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard
- **FCA (UK Financial Conduct Authority)**: UK financial services regulations
- **Basel III/IV**: International banking regulations with AI risk management requirements
- **MiFID II**: European financial markets regulations

**Government Regulatory Framework:**
- **FISMA (Federal Information Security Management Act)**: US government security requirements
- **FedRAMP**: US government cloud security certification
- **EU AI Act**: European Union's comprehensive AI regulation (enforceable August 2026)
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**: US government AI governance standards
- **StateRAMP**: State government security certification
- **ITAR/EAR**: International trade and export controls for defense and intelligence applications

**Cross-Sector Requirements:**
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Service organization controls certification
- **ISO 27001**: International information security management standard
- **ISO 42001**: AI-specific management system standard
- **Data Residency**: Jurisdictional data storage and processing requirements
- **Model Transparency**: Requirements for AI model documentation and explainability
