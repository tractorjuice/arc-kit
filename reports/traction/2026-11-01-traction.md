**The usage signal is cautiously positive.** Three distinct external contributors (chrismckelt, chrisns, johnfelipe) submitted real code this month — not just issues. **The gap between stars and engagement remains wide.** 2,266 stars but only 14 watchers, no confirmed paying/subscribing users, and no external contributor who has returned for a second month (johnfelipe is the closest, with activity across multiple months). The repo is in the "starred and bookmarked" category for most of its audience; converting that audience to regular use will require distribution events beyond GitHub Trending — blog posts, conference talks, or integrations that put ArcKit in the path of architecture practitioners.

**One tactical concern**: the proxy restriction in the automated traction run blocks fork ahead-by analysis and stargazer profiling — the two most actionable signals for identifying high-value adopters. These should be run manually or from a local session with a full PAT.
