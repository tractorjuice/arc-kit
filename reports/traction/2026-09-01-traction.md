That puts the August external contribution count at 8 merged PRs from 3 contributors, which exceeds the total for the prior three months combined. The `terrygzhou` OAA-split PR being independently submitted (and closed as duplicate because the maintainer was building the same thing in-tree) is the clearest signal yet that power users are tracking the roadmap and acting on it. The `johnfelipe` pattern (many issues, 0 PRs) remains the leading conversion indicator to watch: if it tips to a PR in September, it confirms the most prolific external issue reporter has moved to contributor status.

Open issues shrank from 20 to 13 despite the release burst — the maintainer is closing faster than issues are opening, which is a healthy backlog signal. The three remaining `help wanted` issues and the jurisdiction-contribution request (#424) are the clearest entry points for further external contributors.
