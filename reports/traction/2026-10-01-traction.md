## 7. Honest Read

The trending event in April drove 1,327 stars in 6 days, and the repo now sits at 2,252 — meaning ~925 stars arrived organically across the subsequent 5+ months, at a rate that has gradually stabilised around 1–4/day. The star growth alone signals broad awareness, but awareness is not adoption.

The clearest adoption signal this month is `@johnfelipe`: 10 security PRs in a single week, plus 3 open bug/enhancement issues, from someone who has clearly deployed ArcKit in a real environment and found real issues. `@thomas-jardinet`'s French overlay PR (#853) adds a second external contributor fixing domain-specific content, indicating the community overlay model is working beyond just the maintainer. Both are consistent with "actually using" rather than "starred and forgot."

However, **watcher count is only 13** — unchanged territory for an enterprise governance tool that ostensibly runs inside organisations' repos. The low watcher count combined with the declining star rate (42 this month vs ~100/month in Jun–Aug) suggests the post-trending audience is not converting to engaged followers at scale. The fork engagement picture is incomplete (proxy blocked cross-fork compare), but the 4 forks-with-stars and the birhantprkc push-from-yesterday suggest a handful of genuine adopters.

The honest conclusion: ArcKit has crossed from "viral curiosity" into "has a real user community" — but that community is small (low single digits of active external contributors), and the funnel from star to watcher to contributor remains very narrow. The "Show Your Working" campaign launched in September may help; watch whether the Oct star rate recovers toward 3+/day.
