# ArcKit Adopters

Organisations that have told us they use ArcKit.

ArcKit collects no usage data. Nothing phones home, and nothing will, because a governance toolkit should not quietly report on the projects it helps. That means this page is the only record of who uses it, and it only lists organisations that asked to be listed.

## Add your organisation

Open an [**I use ArcKit**](https://github.com/tractorjuice/arc-kit/issues/new?template=adopter.yml) issue. It takes about two minutes.

You choose how you appear:

- **Named**: your organisation's name, with a line about what you use ArcKit for.
- **Anonymous**: your sector only, for example "a central government department". This suits teams whose communications rules don't allow them to endorse a tool publicly.
- **Not listed**: you tell the maintainer, and nobody else sees it.

The issue is public, so your GitHub username is visible whichever you choose. If that's a problem, send the same details on [Discord](https://discord.gg/HsA4Y3hQ4) or through the [LinkedIn group](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/17641034/) instead.

To change or remove a listing, comment on your issue or open a pull request against this file.
