# Ashcombe District Council: Digital and Benefits Governance (extract)

Prepared by the Transformation Office, August 2026.

## Reporting lines

- **Chief Executive**: Margaret Ellery. Accountable to Full Council.
- **Director of Resources**: Daniel Quist. Owns the Revenues and Benefits service and its budget; the Section 151 officer.
- **Head of Digital**: Priya Nandakumar. Chairs the **Design Authority**, which approves architecture decisions and any new supplier.
- **Benefits Service Manager**: Tom Okafor. Service Owner for housing benefits; line-manages 22 caseworkers across two offices.
- **Data Protection Officer**: Lena Marsh. Must sign off any processing of claimant data before go-live.

## Boards

| Board | Chair | Cadence | Decides |
|-------|-------|---------|---------|
| Design Authority | Head of Digital | Fortnightly | Architecture, suppliers, technical standards |
| Benefits Transformation Board | Director of Resources | Monthly | Scope, budget, go-live |
| Information Governance Group | Data Protection Officer | Monthly | DPIA sign-off, retention |

## Known tensions

The Director of Resources has a savings target of £180k a year from the benefits service by 2027-28. Caseworkers have raised, through their union representative, that the last system change was rolled out without training and increased overtime for three months.
