# Brightwater Systems Ltd: Proposal for the Housing Benefits Portal

## Our solution

Brightwater proposes CaseHarbour, a claims case management platform built on open-source components. Save-and-resume is live in a beta with one district council, where 1,400 claims have been started since March. Caseworker evidence requests are designed and will be released in the first quarter of 2027, before this contract's go-live date.

Claim state, decisions and an audit trail are held in PostgreSQL. CaseHarbour has run benefit claims for one other local authority since 2024, handling about 9,000 claims a year.

## Team

Our current delivery team is four people: a delivery lead, a designer and two engineers, all cleared to BPSS. We will grow the team to ten within eight weeks of contract award, and we will sponsor SC clearance for the engineers who need it. Our delivery lead has run two local-authority digital services through GDS-style service assessments.

## Accessibility

An independent auditor tested CaseHarbour's citizen pages against WCAG 2.1 AA in May 2024 and found two issues, both since fixed. We will commission a WCAG 2.2 AA audit from the same auditor before go-live, and our designers test every new page with a screen reader.
