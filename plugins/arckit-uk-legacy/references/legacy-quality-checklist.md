# Legacy assessment quality checklist

## LEGACY -- Legacy IT Assessment

- Headline axes disclose focused starts and contain all score bounds; scaling never changes scores
- Inventoried components have a verified `-components.svg` companion, including when numerical risk is omitted
- Component outcomes use component-scoped findings; system-wide context is not silently inherited, and advisory confidence/ranges never change findings or approval
- Component plotting uses declared numerical intervals and local calibration, not category-centre positions or manual confidence labels; the point and bounds are calculated independently, with missing inputs explicit
- Component confidence is labelled range precision (not probability or government scoring); recorded classifications remain separate and raw calibration is auditable

- Strongest known urgency remains visible beside Unknown outcomes and named blockers
- Evidence qualifications are per use; rejection reasons name records and failed tests
- Approval is content-bound with a documented decision date or remains pending/stale; later sign-off cannot move the assessment evidence cutoff
- Bounds on incomplete numerical inputs are mathematical sensitivity, not completed scores or official red-rated findings
- Generated inventory, policies, dependencies, findings, evidence and action registers pass report verification; narrative claims remain assessor responsibility
- Actions addressing missing coverage or dependencies do not resolve the underlying evidence gaps
- Dated review triggers retain source IDs and remain distinct from delivery dates

- Seven D1–D7 findings per system, with dated evidence, threshold references, rationale and uncertainty
- Classification, policy basis and evidence completeness are separate; Unknown is internal and draft rules are provisional
- Approval has documentary evidence or remains pending; missing principles/requirements create gaps, not a blocked assessment
- D7 reliance is evidenced and qualified under policy; causal paths and provisional origins are retained; cycles invent no Legacy
- Numerical L1–L7 and C1–C6 inputs are independent; L7 is historical incidents, not D7 dependencies
- Helper verification succeeds; Markdown decisions/counts/arithmetic match JSON results, or calculations are explicitly unverified
- Missing numerical inputs stay incomplete; red comparison uses unrounded score; no additional official risk bands
- Actions trace to findings and include ownership, containment/remediation, funding, milestones, dates and closure evidence; unknowns explicit
- Risk acceptance/controls do not silently remediate findings; proposed review and reassessment triggers are documented
- Previous versions preserved; source dates recorded; no current government submission compatibility or universal LEGACY requirement claimed
- Helper-generated results block appears exactly once and `--check-report` succeeds against the enriched JSON; no hand-written duplicate decision/risk tables
- When numerical scoring is enabled, the linked versioned `-risk.svg` exists beside the report and passes `--check-report`; complete scores use unrounded points, incomplete scores use annotated bounding envelopes/range bars rather than estimated points, and all systems are keyed, and only the published red-rated boundary at 16 is shown
- Approval records identify the exact decision/version and named documented approver; operational evidence and another decision's approval cannot approve this one
- System/component evidence scope and whole-system coverage checked; D7 has an independent causal origin and bounded witness paths
- Management gaps and action-plan completeness are visible independently of classification and evidence; completion evidence is applicable, dated and not merely proposed
