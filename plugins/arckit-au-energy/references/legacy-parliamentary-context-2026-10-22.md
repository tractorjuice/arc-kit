# Parliamentary context for legacy assessments

Source: [Rewiring the state: Delivering digital government — Government response](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5902/cmselect/cmsctech/651/report.html).
Published 22 October 2026; response received 7 September 2026. Checked 28 October 2026.

## Relevant distinctions

- **Recommendation 13:** ministers support coordination but describe exploring a taskforce. Its establishment is not evidenced here. Renewed data collection and an action plan are prospective commitments.
- **Recommendation 14:** modernisation budgets were ringfenced through confidential departmental settlements. This does not establish funding for any particular project. The named Technology Modernisation Action Plan was still expected later in 2026.
- **Recommendation 21:** a sourcing strategy was being developed around modularity, interoperability, portability and contractual exits. Ministers declined prescribed supplier-diversification targets.
