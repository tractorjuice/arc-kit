type: regex
pattern: '(?is)^(?=.*\b(?:proposed|waiting|waits)\b)(?=.*\bshown\b)'
target: last_message
---

The reply says that the dish is proposed, or waiting, and that it is to be shown: the owner reads that the dish they set out is recorded but does not count yet, and why. A reply that says the dish is recorded as the business's, with no word of its waiting to be shown and agreed, fails this.
