### Added

- First release of the UK Food Safety Overlay (experimental, community-maintained, England and Wales). It builds a small caterer's food safety documents from the Food Standards Agency's Safer food, better business for caterers pack: a business profile, the five sections of safe methods, the business's own methods, a completion record, and sign-off by a named person. Commands for these arrive in a later change in this series.
