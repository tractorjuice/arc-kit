## LinkedIn Post Body

ArcKit 6.21.0 gives design reviews and vendor scores a second look before they stand.

Before /arckit:hld-review writes a review, three fresh readers check the design, one each for comprehension, principles and requirements. They see the design, the principles and the requirements, and none of the review's working. Every finding they raise gets an outcome in the review. /arckit:dld-review gets the same, with one reader checking the detailed design against the approved HLD.

/arckit:score and /arckit:evaluate now score each bid twice. The second scorer never sees the first scores or the vendor's name. Where the two differ, a moderator decides without being told whose score is whose, and both first scores are kept.
