    Awareness

    A 40-question scenario quiz.

    Practitioner

    You have produced a governed, traceable set of documents and improved them by hand.

    A project you submit, reviewed by a person.

    Expert

    You can lead ArcKit adoption and extend ArcKit.

What a credential is

Each credential is an Open Badges 3.0 credential signed by ArcKit. Anyone can check it at its link, and you can add it to LinkedIn in one click.

It confirms your ArcKit skill. It does not endorse your work or your organisation.
